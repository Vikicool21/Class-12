Drawing

Drawing is a form of visual art that involves creating images on a surface, usually paper, using various tools such as pencils, pens, or charcoal. It allows individuals to express their creativity and imagination.

Singing

Singing is the practice of producing musical sounds with the voice. It involves controlling breath, pitch, and tone to create melodies and harmonies.

Coding

Coding is the process of creating computer programs by writing instructions in a programming language. It involves problem-solving and logical thinking to develop software applications.
